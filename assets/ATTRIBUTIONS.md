# Third-party model asset register

## Animated alien creatures

Both models are original work by vinish.dev and are published under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)**. They are loaded as GLB files with their embedded skeletons and animation clips. Cosmocrush scales and reorients the models for the game.

| Role | Asset | Source page | GLB URL | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alien, standard | Blind Stalker | https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/monsters | https://vinish-models.pages.dev/monsters/blind-stalker.glb | CC BY 4.0 |
| Alien, alternate | Deep Lurker | https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/monsters | https://vinish-models.pages.dev/monsters/deep-lurker.glb | CC BY 4.0 |

### Required credits

- "Blind Stalker" by vinish.dev (https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/), CC BY 4.0
- "Deep Lurker" by vinish.dev (https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/), CC BY 4.0

The pack includes Idle, Walk, Run, Attack, Roar, and Die animations. Walk and Run are in-place clips; Cosmocrush moves the enemy through the world separately.
