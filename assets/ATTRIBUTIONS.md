# Third-party model asset register

## Player marine

The player uses the **Sci-Fi Trooper** by vinish.dev, a skinned humanoid GLB with authored Idle, Walk and Run animation clips. Cosmocrush adds a two-handed rifle pose and muzzle attachment to the rig.

## Animated alien creatures

Both models are original work by vinish.dev and are published under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)**. They are loaded as GLB files with their embedded skeletons and animation clips. Cosmocrush scales and reorients the models for the game.
